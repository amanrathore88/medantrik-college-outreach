Principal's Message

It is with great pride and privilege I welcome you warmly to Swami Shraddhanand College - an institution for higher education with a long and prestigious past. This college was established in 1967 to copiously cater to the education needs of students of North Delhi and adjoining areas at affordable cost and convenience. In the initial years, the college strived to its very best to impart higher education to mostly first generation students.
