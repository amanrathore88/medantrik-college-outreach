Message from the Principal

Warm Greetings from the Principal, Mata Sundri College for Women. Mata Sundri College for Women (MSC) is named after Mata Sundri Ji, an able leader of Khalsa and the consort of Guru Gobind Singh ji. She was the first transformational leader of the Sikhs who donned the leadership role in times of turmoil to guide the Khalsa. In the present times, it is imperative to equip young women with information, requisite skills, and training so that they may excel in the fields they choose and also lead empowered lives. Our objective at MSC is to shape leaders who can steer change and take up personal and professional challenges with ease. We believe in creating an academic environment geared towards excellence...

Prof. Harpreet Kaur
