Principal's Desk

Education is a journey which involves the transformation of students into responsible citizens. This process further branches into components like character building and acquisition of moral integrity through the absorption of positive knowledge. We, at Rajdhani college, do not believe in mere accumulation of knowledge through the implementation of curriculum. It continues beyond and after a class is over. Theoretically, a classroom is a mirror which reflects the personality of a teacher, it shows a society its true shadow. Hence, a crucial and close examination of the process involved in the exchange of knowledge is required and I firmly believe that it can be done only when we dissect the relationship between education and social structures.
