FAQ’s

What courses are offered at Indraprastha College for Women?

Indraprastha College offers undergraduate and postgraduate courses across disciplines such as Arts, Science, Commerce. For detailed course lists,please refer to the Academics section on thewebsite.

How can I apply for admission?

Admissions are conducted through the University of Delhi’s centralized online portal. Candidates must fill the application form during the admission cycle and submit the required documents as per university guidelines.

What are the eligibility criteria for admission?

Eligibility criteria vary by course. Generally, candidates must have completed their 10+2 examination from a recognized board with the minimum required marks for the desired program.

When does the admission process start?

Admission usually begins after the declaration of the Class 12 results, typically around June-July. Please check the official website regularly for updates and important dates.

What documents are required during admission?

  • Marksheet of qualifying examination (10+2)
  • Transfer Certificate/Leaving Certificate
  • Identity Proof (Aadhar Card, Passport, etc.)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • Any other document specified by the university

Is there any provision for reserved category students?

Yes, Indraprastha College follows the reservation policies as per the Government of India and University of Delhi norms for SC/ST/OBC/EWS and PwD candidates.

Navigating the Chronicle

The story of Indraprastha College for Women is the gradual realization of the vision of empowerment and educational process that began in the heart of Old Delhi.

On May 20, in 1904, the institution took its first steps in the form of Indraprastha Putri Pathshala, welcoming the first batch of students into a rented building in Gali Anar, Chandni Chowk.
