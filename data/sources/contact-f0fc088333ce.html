Principal's Message

As I take on the responsibility of leading this remarkable institution, it is a moment of profound humility and an overwhelming sense of homecoming. Long before I had the privilege of shaping this institution, this institution shaped me. As a daughter of the sacred soil of LSR, I look forward to making strong and steadfast contributions to nation building, as generations of women at LSR have historically done through their unwavering focus on Lakshya, Sadhna, Rachna.

At LSR, we have a long-standing history of empowering young women to assume leadership roles. To ensure that such success becomes the norm rather than the exception, we must look closely at the current climate of women’s leadership in India. The new Women Leadership in Corporate India 26 report shows a widening gap between intent, ambition and outcome. Only 10% of women currently occupy leadership roles; 1% board-level positions. The problem is not entry or aspiration but progression.

About LSR

Lady Shri Ram College for Women, has long been recognized as a premier institution of higher learning for women in India. Established by the late Sir Shri Ram in the memory of his wife in 1956, LSR has always been, restless and tireless in fulfilling his vision of ‘More education. More education for Women. And more education for more women.’

A centre for academic excellence and achievement, LSR today is one of the finest institutions in the country offering sixteen courses in Social Sciences, Humanities and Commerce, while also offering a B.Sc. Programme in Statistics, and professional courses like Elementary Education and Journalism. Be it academics, extracurricular or co-curricular activities, LSR has proven itself time and again. We have been ranked among the top in nation-wide surveys and have received an A++ grade in the second cycle of the NAAC accreditation process.

  • Lady Shri Ram College for Women proudly celebrates the remarkable achievements of its alumnae at the Asian Games 2026 in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. Aashima Ahlawat, Philosophy, Class of 2022, clinched silver in the Women’s Trap Team event, alongside Neeru Dhanda and Manisha Keer, with the Indian team recording 328 points.  Adding another medal to LSR’s sporting legacy, Maheshwari Chauhan, Class of 2017, secured bronze in the Women’s Skeet Team event, representing India alongside Raiza Dhillon and Parinaaz Dhaliwal. Their journeys from the classrooms and campus of LSR to the Asian Games podium stand as a celebration of talent, perseverance and the pursuit of excellence. 
  • Lady Shri Ram College for Women proudly congratulates Krishna Pathak and Arya Dixit, B.A. (Hons.) Political Science, 3rd Year, on winning the Hindi iteration of the 3rd Shri Arun Jaitley Memorial National Debate, held at Shri Ram College of Commerce (SRCC) on 23 September 2026. Competing against 100+ teams from across India, Krishna and Arya emerged victorious and secured the ₹75,000 cash prize. The awards were presented by Vice-President of India C. P. Radhakrishnan, who highlighted the importance of critical thinking, questioning and reasoned dialogue in universities. Their achievement is a proud moment for LSR and reflects the College’s enduring tradition of intellectual engagement and thoughtful discourse.
  • It gives us immense pride to share that Lady Shri Ram College for Women was mentioned and appreciated at the Independence Day celebration event today at the Vice Chancellor’s office, Delhi University for maintaining cleanliness and greenery on campus. This appreciation Prize came as a result of the inspection conducted under the Swachhta Pakhwada Abhiyan, Delhi University from 1st August 2026 to 14th August 2026. Read More
  • LSR is proud to celebrate alumna Ms. Deepti Gaur Mukerjee, Batch of 1986–89, on being entrusted with the additional charge of Chairperson, University Grants Commission (UGC). Currently serving as Secretary, Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education, her appointment is yet another proud milestone in the remarkable journey of an LSR alumna. From the classrooms of LSR to a position of leadership in India’s higher education landscape, she continues to carry forward the LSR legacy of excellence, leadership and service.
  • Dr Meeta Pandit, Batch of 1995, was honoured with the Femina Achievers 2026 Award for Music. She was recognised for decades spent carrying the Gwalior Gharana tradition of Hindustani classical music to audiences across India and the world.
  • LSR is proud to celebrate alumna Justice Meenakshi M. Rai, Political Science class of ’86, on her appointment as the 48th Chief Justice of Patna High Court. In 2015, she made history as the first woman judge of Sikkim High Court. Today, she continues to break barriers with integrity and service.
  • LSR congratulates it’s alumna Aditi Mishra, Class of 1982, for receiving an honorary doctorate from Wesleyan College, Georgia, USA, in recognition of her exemplary contributions to the field of education.
  • India Post Honors Delhi’s Leading Women’s Colleges. LSR received the honour of being selected as one of four leading Delhi women’s colleges to be represented on a special miniature sheet containing a set of commemorative stamps released by India Post. The stamp celebrates the institution’s legacy of excellence, education, and women’s empowerment. It is a tribute to LSR’s role in nurturing confident women leaders, bold thinkers, and change makers. The stamp was unveiled by Ms. Vandita Kaul, Secretary (Posts), and Ms. Smriti Sharan, Joint Secretary, Department of Rural Development on March 25, 2026.
  • In 2025-26, 10 students of LSR cleared the UPSC CSE 2025- Surabhi Yadav, Ifra S. Ansari, Devanshi Kaura, Maurvie Tiwari, Kashish Karla, Navneet Kaur, Gauri Bansal, Shreya Gupa, Smith Sabu, Kritika Chaudhary.
  • LSR won the title in the Women’s College Category basketball tournament at the Delhi Khel Mahakumbh 2026, defeating Shri Ram College of Commerce in the final. Initiated by the Government of Delhi, the Mahakumbh began on February 13, 2026, at Chhatrasal Stadium.
  • In March 2026, Enactus LSR members -Aanya Gupta, Purvi Kansal, and Vanshika Khanna placed first at the ENS Sustainability Case Competition organized by the Indian Institute of Management, Bangalore, competing against 1400+ participants.

    The National Commission for Women has set up a dedicated round the clock (24×7) Helpline number for women in distress.
