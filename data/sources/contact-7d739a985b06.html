Principal's Desk

Jesus and Mary College, New Delhi, is more than just an institution it is a living legacy of St. Claudine Thévenet, whose vision of compassion, forgiveness, education, and transformation continues to inspire every corner of our campus. Rooted in a rich heritage of value-based education and academic excellence, the college stands not merely as a structure of concrete, but as a vibrant academic fraternity dedicated to the holistic development and empowerment of women.
