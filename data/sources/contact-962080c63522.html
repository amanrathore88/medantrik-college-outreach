Education, designed the ISH way

We are Liberal Hospitality Education.

At ISH, we blend hospitality, business, and leadership with critical thinking, adaptability, and a global perspective.

Our undergraduate, postgraduate, professional, and experiential programs are offered in academic alliance with Les Roches and through École Ducasse India, bringing international expertise, global standards, and meaningful career pathways to every ISH experience.

In alliance with Les Roches Switzerland, this program blends hospitality and business education, equipping graduates with global pathways, interdisciplinary and life skills.

Bachelor in Global Business & Entrepreneurial Leadership

Discover the ISH Bachelor's in Global Business & Entrepreneurial Leadership. A four-year program that develops adaptable, future-ready leaders with skills in strategy, finance, venture building, and global business.

B.A (Hons.) in Culinary Arts

At École Ducasse India, Gurugram, students gain culinary mastery with business skills, blending technique, management, and entrepreneurship for global F&B success.

Postgraduate programs

View All

PGCM in Hospitality Management

This AICTE-approved, one-year program at ISH, in alliance with Les Roches, builds leadership in service with progression to the Les Roches Switzerland MSc in International Hospitality Management.

PGCM in Real Estate Finance

This AICTE-approved, one-year program at ISH builds expertise in finance and asset management for hospitality, service, and real estate ventures.

Postgraduate Certificate in Management (AICTE Approved) – Bakery and Pastry Arts

This AICTE-approved, one-year program at École Ducasse India equips graduates and career switchers to master advanced bakery and pastry craft, building expertise for F&B ventures

Diploma programs

View All

Culinary Arts Diploma— École Ducasse India × École Ducasse Paris

The Culinary Arts Diploma is a 12-month journey that begins at École Ducasse India at ISH in Gurugram and continues at École Ducasse Paris.

Diploma in Culinary Arts — École Ducasse India × At-Sunrice GlobalChef Academy

The Diploma in Culinary Arts is a 16-month, two-country learning journey delivered by École Ducasse India at ISH in academic articulation with At-Sunrice GlobalChef Academy, Singapore.

Diploma in Pastry & Bakery — École Ducasse India × At-Sunrice GlobalChef Academy

The Diploma in Pastry & Bakery is a 16-month articulation pathway by École Ducasse India at ISH and At-Sunrice GlobalChef Academy, Singapore.

Your Success, Powered by Our Network.

At ISH, your career journey is personal. The Centre for Career Development (CCD), in collaboration with the Centre for Life Skills (CLS), ensures that your strengths, skills, and aspirations align with the right career opportunities. We prioritize company-student fit, matching you with organizations that foster professional growth in every aspect. Through internships, mentorships, and full-time opportunities with leading companies across industries, CCD helps you build a future designed around your ambitions.

Step into an Extraordinary Campus

The ISH campus is built around the student lifecycle. From kitchen training zones to business spaces, lounges to ideation corners, every area drives interaction, reflection, and purpose. Academic zones encourage collaboration, culinary floors mirror professional kitchens, and breakout spaces double as idea rooms and mentorship pods. The campus is LEED-certified, zero-plastic, and located in Gurugram’s hospitality and business corridor, offering daily exposure to global brands, ventures, and industry leaders.
