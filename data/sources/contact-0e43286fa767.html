Our Programs

JK Business School offers PGDM and BBA programs focused on innovation, leadership, and global exposure for future-ready professionals.

2 Year Program - PGDM

AICTE-approved Post Graduate Diploma in Management designed to build future-ready leaders through industry-aligned learning, global exposure, and skill-based pedagogy. Specializations offered:

(Member – International Accreditation AMDISA SAQS Accredited, F.118 (Vol.III) I.M.2025-2030)

  • Marketing
  • Finance
  • Operations & Supply Chain Management
  • Information Technology
  • Human Resources (HR)

PGDM Approved by AICTE, Ministry of HRD, Govt. Of India
Member of Association of Management Development Institutions In South Asia
(International Accreditation AMDISA SAQS Accredited)
Vide Membership No. (F.118 (Vol.III) I.M.2025-2030)

Explore PGDM

3 Year Program - BBA

The Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) affiliated to Gurugram university is an undergraduate course that equips students with the essential knowledge, skills, and mindset needed to succeed in the competitive world of business. Designed to foster innovation and leadership, the program provides a solid grounding in management principles while offering real-world exposure through internships and industry projects.

  • Entrepreneurship & Leadership
  • International Business
  • Marketing & Digital Strategies
  • Finance & Banking
  • Business Analytics
Explore BBA

2 Year Program - PGDM in Data Analytics and Business Intelligence

The PGDM – Certification in Data Analytics & Business Intelligence is designed to develop analytical and strategic business leaders. It is offered in collaboration with ISDC and the Institute of Analytics (UK), provides international certification and hands-on learning in key data domains. Core modules:

(Member – International Accreditation AMDISA SAQS Accredited, F.118 (Vol.III) I.M.2025-2030)

  • Business Modelling using Excel
  • R Programming for Analytics
  • DBMS with SQL
  • Python Programming
  • Data Visualization (Tableau/Power BI)
  • Machine Learning with R/Python

(Accredited by Institute of Analytics, United Kingdom)
ISDC Certification in Collaboration with IOA focusing on Business Intelligence & Data Driven Decision Making

Explore PGDM DABI

2 Year Program - PGDM in Fintech

The PGDM-Fintech in partnership with ACCA ,UK is designed to integrate traditional financial principles with modern technological advancements in the finance sector. Through this program, students will gain a deep understanding of accounting, financial management and emerging technologies such as fintech, digital banking and blockchain. It is perfect for those aspiring to pursue a career in the evolving world of finance, with a focus on technology-driven solutions for financial operations.

  • Accounting & Financial Management
  • FinTech Ecosystem
  • Digital Payments & Cryptocurrencies
  • Blockchain Technology
  • Banking & Financial Markets

(Powered by ACCA, United Kingdom)
ISDC Certificate & Certification in Fintech from ACCA*
