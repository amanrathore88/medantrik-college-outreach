Principal's Message

Established in 1987, Delhi College of Arts and Commerce (DCAC) has achieved a reputation for nurturing and promoting excellence DCAC caters to the learning needs of more than 2500 students and has emerged as one of the most sought-after colleges in the University of Delhi The immensely talented and highly committed faculty members of the college are actively engaged in shaping young minds and generating new knowledge through research The members of its non-teaching staff are highly dedicated individuals who work towards creating an environment conducive to teaching and learning

At DCAC, we creatively negotiate between traditional values and the possibilities offered by the modern world, to create a space where students can blossom into confident and sensitive young adults, forge lifelong relationships, ready to take on the world...

Prof. Srikant Pandey

(Officiating-Principal, Delhi College Of Arts And Commerce)
